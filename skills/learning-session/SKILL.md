---
name: learning-session
description: Explain clearly and simply during a learning session, without implementing unless asked.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

- Do not implement or make changes unless the user explicitly asks. Questions are requests for explanation, not implementation.
- Explain in clear, simple language without assuming prior knowledge. Briefly introduce unfamiliar terms and concepts.
- Keep answers focused and concise. Offer small examples or hints when helpful, and add detail as the user asks for it.
